Most entries on this map were compiled from APT records and from publicly available information about your practice — not from anything you filled in. That means an entry can be wrong, out of date, or simply something you would rather not be part of. This form is how you fix that.

You can have your listing taken off the map, and you do not have to say why. Choose Remove my listing entirely below, tell us which entry is yours, and send. No reason, no justification, no negotiation. We may reply once to confirm the request came from you or your firm; we will not ask you to explain it, and we will not try to talk you out of it.

Removals and corrections are made by hand and take effect the next time the map data is published.

What happens to what you send

  • Pressing the button does not upload anything. It opens a message in your own email program, addressed to the project maintainer, with your answers written into the body. You can read and edit it before sending, and nothing leaves your computer until you press send in your email program.
  • Your email address will be visible to the maintainer, because you are sending an email. It is used to process and, if needed, confirm your request. It is not published on the map.
  • A removal request is acted on by deleting the entry from the published map data. What you wrote here is kept only as long as needed to carry the removal out and to make sure the entry is not re-added by a later research pass; the fact that an entry was removed on request is retained for that purpose.
  • Corrections change what is published: name, firm, city, state/province, country, website, firm-level public contact, specialty tags, disaster tags, and documented projects. Every published tag keeps a source link.
  • Never published: home addresses, personal email addresses, mobile numbers, and any claim without a public source.
  • This page loads no fonts, scripts, trackers or analytics from anywhere. There is no cookie and no logging.
