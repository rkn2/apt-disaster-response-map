APT Disaster-Response & Rebuild Professionals

Use this form if you are a preservation professional or firm with disaster-response or historic-rebuild experience and you are not currently on the map. If you are already listed and something is wrong, or you want to be taken off, use the update or removal form instead.

Every submission is reviewed before it appears, and every tag on the map carries a source. Specialty and disaster-response tags are only assigned when there is publicly checkable evidence — a project page, a publication, a news story, a workshop you taught, a committee role. Please give us links. A listing with location and discipline only, and no disaster tags, is a normal and honest outcome; it is better than a tag we cannot back up. Submitting this form does not guarantee a listing.

If your email program did not open

This page has no server. It hands your answers to your own email program. If nothing happened when you pressed the button — which is common on webmail-only setups such as Gmail in a browser without a mail handler registered — copy the text below and email it yourself to , with the subject line shown.

What happens to what you send

  • Pressing the button does not upload anything. It opens a message in your own email program, addressed to the project maintainer, with your answers written into the body. You can read and edit it before sending, and nothing leaves your computer until you press send in your email program.
  • Your email address will be visible to the maintainer, because you are sending an email. It is used for follow-up about your listing and is not published on the map.
  • What may be published on the map: name, firm, individual/firm, city, state/province, country, website, firm-level public contact, specialty tags, disaster tags, documented projects, and the source links behind each tag.
  • What is never published: home addresses, personal email addresses, mobile numbers, and any tag we cannot support with a public source.
  • This page loads no fonts, scripts, trackers or analytics from anywhere. There is no cookie and no logging.
  • Being listed is voluntary. You can ask to be corrected or removed at any time, with no reason required, via the update or removal form.
